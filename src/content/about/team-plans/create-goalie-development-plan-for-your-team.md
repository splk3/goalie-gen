# Create a Goalie Development Plan for Your Team

Goalie Gen’s team plan generator helps coaches and goalies produce a structured, editable `.docx` team goalie development plan in minutes.

Key capabilities include:

- team identity inputs (team name, logo, website, mission/motto, age group)
- configurable goalie support options (mentor support, evaluations, starter sections)
- calendar planning tools for team events and per-event details
- optional shot tracker support for game entries
- reusable resource and planning structure aligned with USA Hockey goaltending development themes

### Quick walkthrough

1. Click **Generate a Goalie Development Plan for Your Team** to open the generator.
2. Enter team profile details and age group, then optionally upload a team logo.
3. Configure goalie development options and starter content toggles.
4. If desired, add calendar events and control which event types include detailed entries.
5. Generate and download the team plan, then tailor placeholder sections for your specific season.

Use the generated plan as an internal team operating document for coaches and goalies, with hockey director coordination as needed.
