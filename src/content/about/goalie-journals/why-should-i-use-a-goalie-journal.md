Goalies carry a unique mental and emotional load. Every save (and every goal against) is visible, pressure can be constant, and confidence can swing quickly without a process to reset and refocus on things the goalie can control.

A journal gives goalies that process.

Used well, it helps each goalie:

- take ownership of personal development instead of waiting for feedback only at practices or games
- set goals that are measurable and within their control
- track emotions, preparation habits, and performance patterns over time
- connect mistakes to learning actions, not just outcomes

Because every goalie develops at a different pace and with a different style, personalization is essential. The journal is designed to be used by each goalie with support from coaches, family, and mentors.
