# Create a Goalie Development Plan for Your Club

Goalie Gen’s club development plan generator helps your organization build a professional, editable `.docx` club goalie development plan in minutes.

You can quickly configure:

- club identity details (name, website, motto/mission, optional logo)
- automatic team color selection from the uploaded logo, with manual override
- club-level goalie support details (practice/training model, equipment support, mentorship, evaluations, goalie discounts)
- optional starter sections for introduction, season goals, equipment, and external resources

### Quick walkthrough

1. Click **Generate a Goalie Development Plan for Your Club** to open the generator.
2. Enter your club details and optionally upload a logo, then crop the image as needed.
3. If you upload a logo, the tool will detect and pre-fill primary and secondary colors. Change colors as needed.
4. Complete the goalie training/support options to reflect your current or target program model.
5. Choose which starter sections to include.
6. Select **Generate**, then **Download** your Word document.
7. Open the document in Word, Google Docs, or your editor of choice.
8. Fill out any placeholders and customize the plan to meet the specific needs and goals of your club.
9. When ready, post the Club Development Plan on your website and share it with your families and players so they know how dedicated you are to making your goalies the very best!

The generated plan gives your club a strong first draft that can be shared with board members, coaches, and families right away!
