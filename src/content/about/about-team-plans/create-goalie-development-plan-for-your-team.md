# Create a Goalie Development Plan for Your Team

Goalie Gen’s team plan generator helps coaches and goalies produce a structured, editable Word document team goalie development plan in minutes.

Key capabilities include:

- team information inputs (team name, logo, website, mission/motto, age group)
- configurable goalie support options (mentor support, evaluations, starter content sections)
- calendar planning tools for team events and per-event details, including `.ics` file import from online calendars, monthly view option, and per-event planning/note taking space for each event type
- optional shot tracker space for taking in-game stats and notes
- reusable resource and planning structure aligned with USA Hockey goaltending development themes

## Quick walkthrough

1. Click **Generate a Goalie Development Plan for Your Team** here or on the homepage to open the generator.
2. Enter team details and age group, then optionally upload a team logo.
3. If uploading a logo, crop the logo and confirm/select team colors.
4. Configure goalie development options and starter content toggles.
5. If desired, add calendar events and control which event types include detailed entries.
6. Generate and download the team plan
7. Open the plan in Word, upload to Google Docs, or use your editor of choice to complete placeholder sections and make any desired changes.
8. Export or print your plan for use during the season.

Use the generated plan as an internal team operating document for coaches and goalies, with hockey director coordination as needed.
