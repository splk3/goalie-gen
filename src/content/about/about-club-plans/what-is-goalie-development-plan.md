# What Is a Goalie Development Plan?

A club-level goalie development plan is the organization’s shared roadmap for how it recruits, trains, supports, and retains goalies across all teams at all age groups.

At minimum, a complete plan should define:

- the club’s goalie development philosophy and high-level season goals
- goalie-specific benefits and support commitments (coaching access, practice structure, video, evaluations, discounts, mentorship)
- technical development priorities and progression expectations
- baseline equipment requirements and safety expectations
- progress-tracking and evaluation cadence and details
- club-level goalie development contacts and their role in the organization

A development plan at the club level is important because it creates alignment among directors, coaches, goalies, and families, improves consistency across teams, and makes long-term goalie development repeatable year after year.
Goalies must be recruited, developed, and retained if your club does not want to constantly scramble to field teams and compete at the goalie position.
