### 8U Starter Content

**Goalie Rotation**

All players will have a chance to play goalie in practices and in games.
We will utilize quick-change gear to enable mutiple goalies to rotate in net in a single game or practice.
Goalie rotations will be coordinated by team coaches to make sure all players can try goalie and 
that players who show more interest can get additional rotations, as availability allows.

### 10U Starter Content

**Goalie Rotation**

At 10U, USA Hockey recommends splitting periods between your goalies.
At the halfway mark of each period, switch your goalies, and rotate who plays the first vs. second half of periods.
If you have more than 2 goalies or have goalies who want to skate out,
rotate your goalies so they can skate out when not in net.

*Note:* All teams and goalies are different, so talk to your goalies and work with them on a rotational schedule that works for them and the team.
Using shorter shifts at younger ages can significantly reduce stress on goalies.
Having time on the bench also gives coaches and goalies a chance to communicate and get feedback during games.

### 12U Starter Content

**Goalie Rotation**

12U is a good time to start working the "shift" length of goalies to a full period.
USA Hockey recommends switching goalies each period during a game,
and rotating which goalie starts/closes each game.

*Note:* All teams and goalies are different, so talk to your goalies and work with them on a rotational schedule that works for them and the team.
Using shorter shifts at younger ages can significantly reduce stress on goalies.
Having time on the bench also gives coaches and goalies a chance to communicate and get feedback during games.

### 14U Starter Content

**Goalie Rotation**

At 14U, USA Hockey recommends switching goalies at the midway point of each game.
Rotate which goalie starts vs. closes out each game.

*Note:* All teams and goalies are different, so talk to your goalies and work with them on a rotational schedule that works for them and the team.
Using shorter shifts at younger ages can significantly reduce stress on goalies. 
Having time on the bench also gives coaches and goalies a chance to communicate and get feedback during games.

### 16U and older Starter Content

**Goalie Rotation**

USA Hockey recommends that, at 16U and older, players should be playing complete games.
They recommend rotating goalies through regular season games to make sure players that come to the rink get to play hockey.
For days with double headers (such as at tournaments), its a good idea to let one goalie play the first game, and the other goalie play the second game.

*Note:* All teams and goalies are different, so talk to your goalies and work with them on a rotational schedule that works for them and the team.
