# Helpful Resources

## Page Content

### The Goaltending Skills Cycle

Every save requires you to maintain each spoke of the save cycle, or you will have to scramble to compensate, and making the save will be more difficult.

Every save starts with the your stance, allowing you to react, skating to follow the play, and maintaining strong positioning.

Angle, Depth, and Square. If you are in good position (and still good stance!), you will be ready to make the appropriate save, control the rebound, and recover back into a strong stance, ready to start the cycle all over again.

### The Goaltending Skills Pyramid

Each of the fundamental skills of goaltending builds upon the others.

Each stage cannot be learned successfully until you are strong in the ones below it.

Don't skip the bottom levels; they are your foundation and enable you to achieve your goals of making saves and winning games for your team.

### Coach Z's Zone Map

Use this handy zone map to remind yourself of where to be with respect to the puck location.

Use the crease map to show where your feet should be with respect to your location in the crease and puck location.

This will help you know when and how to transition in and out of the post, and how to manage depth based on where the play is (and where it is going).

Every goalie has their own style - this is not a strict set of rules. Use them as guidelines to help you think about how to approach each play and try new strategies and techniques to see what works for you.
