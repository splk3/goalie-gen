## How to Improve Every Day

- **Use this journal** to track your goals, feelings, worries, and successes during the season. You can look back later and will be encouraged by your progress!

### Maximize Growth at Practice

- **Work hard at every practice.** You have limited ice time each week, so use every opportunity as a chance to get better.
- **Compete with game intensity** on every shot.
- **Pay attention to directions for the drill.** Even if the instructions are for the players, know what skills they are trying to learn so you can either 1) better defend the against it, or 2) better work with your defense
- **Follow your saves** like you would in a game. Build good habits!
- **Give yourself breaks.** If you feel yourself losing focus, step away from the drill for a moment. A game isn't dozens of shots in a row without any resets.  Don't build bad habits!
- **Follow pucks** in to your body to make the save, and follow them out on rebounds.
- **Practice good rebound control** at all times. Consistency builds proficiency.
- **Learn when you get scored on.** Then you can try something new next time to change the outcome. This will help you bounce back in games when goals are scored.
- **Practice communication.** Be vocal and talk to your defense like you were in a game.
- **HAVE FUN!** The key to being successful is to have passion for what you do and embrace the unique challenges and rewards that goaltending has to offer.

### Maximize Growth During Games

- **Arrive early** and be prepared to play, both mentally and physically.
- **Warm-up**: participate in the team warm-up and perform your personal goalie warm-up.
- **Be supportive of your team**, just like you wish them to be supportive of you.
- **Be respectful** of the other team, coaches, officials, and rink staff.
- **Be level headed and have a short memory.** Do your best to approach each game and each shot with the unconditional confidence you've built by showing up every day and working hard at each practice. You will get scored on, but that doesn't stop you from making the next save.
- **Control the pace of the game** by catching or covering pucks, when needed.
- **Remember your value**. You have the ability to inspire your team, you are a leader, and your team will look to you to help set the tone of the game.
