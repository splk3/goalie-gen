## How to Use This Journal

Use this journal to prepare for practices and games, record what you notice, and track your progress throughout the season.

**Enter your season goals.** Your goals should be measurable and achievable. Goals should be within your control whenever possible, so no matter how the season is going or how good or bad your team is, you will have the ability to work towards your goals. 

- _Try_ "I will arrive early and be mentally and physically prepared before every game." _and not_ "I will win 75% of the games I start"
- _Try_ "I will practice good rebound control at every practice" _and not_ "I will stop giving up goals off of rebounds."

**Consistently.** 

**Complete the End of Season Review.** Self-assessments aren't always fun, but this is your chance to give yourself credit for a season of hard work. Be honest about where you need to improve, take ownership of your offseason training, and revisit these notes before next season to set your new goals.
