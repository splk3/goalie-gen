## How to Use This Journal

Use this journal to prepare for practices and games, record what you notice, and track your progress throughout the season.

**Enter your season goals.** Your goals should be measurable and achievable. Goals should be within your control whenever possible, so no matter how the season is going or how good or bad your team is, you will have the ability to work towards your goals.

- Use "I will arrive early and be mentally and physically prepared before every game." and not "I will win 75% of the games I start"
- Use "I will practice good rebound control at every practice" _and not_ "I will stop giving up goals off of rebounds."

**Get to the details in event entries.** Before each event, fill out the Event Details and "Before" section. After the event, fill out the "After" section. Try to get to get beyond the "what" and into the "why". Take control of the outcome by identifying preparation you can do to help achieve your goals for the day.

- _What am I feeling and why?_ Instead of just "I am feeling anxious", try "I am feeling anxious because I want to feel happy about the game and the other team beat us last time."
- _What are my goals for today?_ Instead of "I want to have a shutout", try "I want to be proud of how I played because I gave my team a change to win."
- _How will I prepare to help meet my goals?_ Instead of "I will try my best", try "I will control the pace of the game by freezing loose pucks like I've been practicing."

**Complete the End of Season Review.** Self-assessments aren't always fun. Instead, use the review to give yourself credit for a season of hard work. Be proud of your progress, be honest about where you need to improve, take ownership of your offseason training, and revisit these notes before next season to set your new goals.
