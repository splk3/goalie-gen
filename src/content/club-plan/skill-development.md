## Skill Development Areas

### Fundamental Goalie Stance and Skating Skills

Skating is key to a goalie's ability to follow the play and make saves.
Most of the work to make a save is accomplished before the shot.
This requires proper stance and skating technique.
All goalies at all age groups will consistently focus on these base skills.

USA Hockey publishes a [guide to goaltending basics](https://www.usahockeygoaltending.com/page/show/866192-goaltender-basics) that includes descriptions and demonstrations of the basics of goalie stance and movement, including the following skills:

- Stance
- Positioning
- T-Push
- Shuffle
- C-cut
- Butterfly
- Full Recovery

### The Goaltending Fundamental Skills Cycle

These are the 6 core fundamentals of goaltending as established by USA Hockey.
Each save requires the goalie to cycle through these skills to get into position, make the save, and get ready for the next one.

![Goaltending Fundamental Skills Cycle](/images/drill-design/goaltending-skills-cycle.png)

- **Stance**: be ready for the play and ready to make a save or skate to get into a better position
- **Skating**: have the skill and technique to be able to skate yourself into position
- **Positioning**: Stay on Angle, maintain proper Depth, and stay Square to the puck
- **Save Selection**: make the appropriate save using the appropriate part of the body/equipment
- **Rebound Control**: put the puck in a safe location to minimize danger and follow-up shots
- **Recovery**: minimize time to re-acquire your stance, so you are ready to make the next play
